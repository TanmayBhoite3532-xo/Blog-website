YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy, in plain English.

Last updated October 8, 2026

This demonstration site does not send personal information to a server. Search and article filters run in your browser. Newsletter and contact forms show a local confirmation only; they do not add you to a mailing list or deliver a message.

Information you enter

Form fields are used only to validate the information you type during that visit. This static site does not store submitted form data. If a production newsletter or contact service is connected in the future, this policy should be updated to name that provider and explain its data practices.

Cookies and analytics

No analytics or advertising cookies are set by this site. Third-party font files may be requested from Google Fonts when the page loads.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
